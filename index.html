How to play ✕

Goal: automate everything and finish Omega Research to win.

Camera: WASD / arrows / middle-drag to pan, mouse wheel to zoom.

Mine by hand: click & hold on an ore patch.

Build: pick a machine from the hotbar (or keys 1–8), R to rotate, click to place (drag to lay conveyors), Q or right-click to cancel.

Remove: right-click a machine (items are refunded).

Machines: click one to open it. Drills and furnaces burn coal — feed them with grabber arms or by hand. Grabbers pick up from the tile behind them and drop to the tile in front (the arrow shows the drop side).

Belt lanes: every conveyor carries two independent lanes, side by side. Grabbers and drills drop onto the right-hand lane; grabbers pick up whichever lane has an item waiting closest to the front.

Belt Splitter (research Fast Logistics): a 2-tile machine that pulls from belts behind it and alternates its output, round-robin, between the two belts ahead — handy for balancing one belt into two.

Tunnel Belt (research Fast Logistics): place one, then place a second facing the same way up to 4 tiles ahead to link them as an entrance/exit pair — items ride underground and pop out the far end after a short delay, letting belts cross under other belts or machines.

Research: craft Basic Tomes, feed them into a Study Table with a grabber, then pick a technology in the Tech panel (T).

Panels: E crafting, T research, Esc closes.

Victory!

You automated research itself. The forge must grow.
